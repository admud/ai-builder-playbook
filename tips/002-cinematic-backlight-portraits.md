# Cinematic Backlight Portrait Prompt Playbook

Turn one reference portrait into three consistent, ultra-realistic editorial
images using window light, sheer curtains, and a glowing studio panel.

[Watch the Instagram demo](https://www.instagram.com/p/Db6D7v4SN8l/).

Use only reference photos you own or have permission to use. Image models can
still introduce identity drift, so inspect facial structure, skin texture,
hands, clothing, and accessories before publishing.

## What You Will Make

Three vertical 4:5 portraits with a shared premium visual language:

1. A seated portrait against a bright rectangular window
2. A standing portrait beside glowing sheer curtains
3. A side-profile portrait against a rectangular studio light

## Tools

- A reference-image-capable AI image generator or editor
- One to three clear photos of the subject
- An image viewer for full-resolution quality control

## Recommended Workflow

1. Choose a sharp, unfiltered reference photo with the full face visible.
2. Upload the reference image in the same request as the prompt.
3. Generate each concept separately at a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio.
4. Compare every result with the reference at full resolution.
5. Regenerate any image with facial drift, malformed hands, invented logos,
   or plastic-looking skin.
6. Export the approved images at a consistent resolution and color treatment.

## 1. Seated Window Backlight Portrait

```text
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic seated backlight portrait using the uploaded reference person as the main subject. Replace the person in the scene completely with the uploaded reference person while preserving their exact face, hairstyle, beard/stubble, skin tone, age, body structure, facial proportions, and overall identity.

Scene: the subject is seated on a low bench or window ledge in front of a large bright rectangular window. The surrounding interior is dark and minimal, with the window acting as the main light source behind him. The bright background creates a soft glowing halo and dramatic silhouette effect.

Pose: the subject is sitting in a relaxed, thoughtful pose, leaning slightly forward with elbows resting on the knees. Hands are loosely clasped between the legs, shoulders relaxed, head slightly turned to the side, looking away from camera with a calm, introspective expression.

Outfit: premium light-toned knit sweater or cream sweatshirt, matching light trousers, soft textured fabric, simple elegant casual styling, no logos.

Lighting: strong white backlight from the window, soft rim light on the hair, shoulders, arms, and knees, face in gentle shadow with realistic detail, natural skin texture, cinematic contrast, warm neutral color grading.

Camera: medium seated portrait, slightly lower or eye-level angle, vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, shallow depth of field, realistic lens feel.

Style: cinematic lifestyle portrait, moody indoor atmosphere, premium editorial photography, realistic shadows, soft film grain, minimal composition.
```

## 2. Sheer-Curtain Backlight Portrait

```text
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic soft backlight portrait using the uploaded reference person as the main subject. Replace the original subject completely with the uploaded reference person while preserving their exact facial features, hairstyle, hairline, hair texture, beard/stubble, skin tone, age, body proportions, and overall identity.

Scene: the subject stands in front of sheer white curtains with strong daylight glowing from behind. The curtains should be softly illuminated, semi-transparent, and textured like linen. The surrounding interior is dark and minimal, creating contrast between the glowing curtains and shadowed room.

Pose: the subject stands slightly angled, one hand gently holding or touching the curtain, the other hand resting casually near the pocket. He looks down and away from camera with a calm, poetic, introspective expression. Body language should feel natural, elegant, and lifestyle-oriented.

Outfit: soft cream or beige knit sweater, matching light trousers, premium monochrome neutral styling, clean fabric texture, no logos.

Lighting: diffused backlight through sheer curtains, soft rim light around hair, shoulders, arm, and side profile, gentle shadows across the face, warm neutral color grading, cinematic softness.

Camera: medium portrait, vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, natural framing, shallow depth of field, realistic photography.

Style: premium lifestyle portrait, soft cinematic light, elegant minimal interior, calm emotional mood, realistic fabric and skin texture.
```

## 3. Glowing-Panel Studio Portrait

```text
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic backlight portrait using the uploaded reference person as the main subject. Replace the person in the scene completely with the uploaded reference person while preserving their exact facial features, hairstyle, hair texture, hairline, skin tone, age, body proportions, facial structure, beard/stubble style, and overall identity.

Scene: a dark minimal studio interior with a large glowing rectangular white light panel directly behind the subject. The background should be almost completely dark, with the bright rectangle creating a dramatic silhouette and strong rim light around the subject’s hair, jawline, shoulders, and hands.

Pose: the subject is standing in a calm, elegant side-profile pose, body slightly angled toward the camera, head turned upward and to the side, looking away from the camera with a thoughtful, confident, introspective expression. His hands are gently clasped near the chest, fingers relaxed, with subtle rings if suitable.

Outfit: dress the subject in a premium minimal cream, beige, or off-white crewneck sweatshirt with soft fabric texture, ribbed collar and cuffs, clean luxury casual styling.

Lighting: extreme backlight from the glowing rectangle, soft rim light around the silhouette, face mostly in shadow but still slightly readable, warm cinematic skin tones, deep shadows, high contrast, soft film grain.

Camera: medium close-up portrait, vertical 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio, shallow depth of field, realistic lens compression, premium editorial photography.

Style: ultra-realistic, cinematic, moody, luxury portrait, minimal studio, dramatic shadows, premium lifestyle photography.
```
